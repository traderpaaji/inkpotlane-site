About Inkpot Lane

Inkpot Lane is an independent, family-run publisher based in India, building a growing catalogue of paperback books for every age and stage of life: children discovering stories, festivals and color; students and curious minds learning new skills; and adults planning, reflecting and building something of their own. Our books are published on Amazon under our imprint, Prabhleen Kaur Publishing.

Our mission

To make useful, beautifully made books for every stage of life: books people actually open, use and finish, whether it is a child's first coloring book, a puzzle that fills a quiet afternoon or a planner that carries a whole year of goals.

Our vision

To become a trusted name on the bookshelf worldwide, with a wide catalogue of books that families and readers return to every season and every year, across coloring, activities and puzzles, learning, journaling, planning and personal growth.

What we stand for

Made to be used. Every page has to earn its place. We would rather publish books that are used every day than books that sit on a shelf.

A book for every reader. Each title is designed for a real reader at a real age and stage, with its own format, level and purpose.

Rooted in story and culture. Our books draw on the stories, festivals and traditions people care about, told with warmth and respect.

Honest by design. What we say about a book is exactly what you will find inside it.

Our shelf today

Coloring books for children aged 4 to 12, built around festivals and stories: Christmas, Diwali and Ganesh Chaturthi.
Goal planners: The Life I'm Building, dated for 2027 or undated.
A trading journal: The Trader I'm Becoming, for keeping a disciplined written record of every trade.

Where we are growing

Our catalogue is expanding into new categories: search-and-find and activity books, puzzle books, learning workbooks for children, and more journals, planners and books on money and personal growth, each one researched and built for the reader it serves.
