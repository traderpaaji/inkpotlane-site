About Inkpot Lane

Inkpot Lane is an independent, family-run publisher based in India. We create paperback books for two kinds of readers: children who are discovering stories, festivals and color, and adults who are building something in their own lives. Our books are published on Amazon under our imprint, Prabhleen Kaur Publishing.

Our mission

To make books people actually finish: books that give a child a story worth coloring, and give an adult a clear place to plan, reflect and follow through.

Our vision

To build a shelf of books that families return to every season and every year, for the festivals they celebrate, the goals they set and the habits they keep, and to become the name readers trust when they want a book that is thoughtfully made and genuinely useful.

What we stand for

Made to be used. Every page has to earn its place. We would rather publish fewer books that are used every day than many that sit on a shelf.

Rooted in story and culture. Our children's books carry the festivals and traditions families care about, told with warmth and respect.

Honest by design. What we say about a book is exactly what you will find inside it.

What we publish

Coloring books for children aged 4 to 12, built around festivals and stories: Christmas, Diwali and Ganesh Chaturthi.
Goal planners: The Life I'm Building, dated for 2027 or undated, for turning one big goal into weekly action.
A trading journal: The Trader I'm Becoming, for keeping a disciplined written record of every trade.
