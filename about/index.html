About Inkpot Lane

Inkpot Lane is a small, family-run independent publisher based in India. We make paperback books that are meant to be used: coloring books for children, and goal planners and a trading journal for grown-ups. On Amazon, our books are published under our imprint name, Prabhleen Kaur Publishing.

How we make the books

Every coloring book is built to the same rules: lines thick enough for small hands, shapes big enough to finish, and one picture to a sheet with nothing printed on the back, so markers never spoil the next page. Each picture is checked page by page before the book goes to print. Festival and religious stories are told gently, the way you would tell them to a young child, and where we simplify a traditional story, the article about it says so.

The planners and the journal are designed page by page to be answered with a pen: fewer decorations, better questions. Numbers such as the 53 weeks of the 2027 planner are worked out from the calendar, not guessed.

How we write the articles

  • Historical, cultural and practical facts are researched and linked to their sources.
  • Every claim about one of our books is checked against its printed pages.
  • We never write that we tested something unless we did, and we never publish invented reviews, testimonials or experts.
  • Anything about the trading journal is about keeping records. It is never investment advice.

Buying our books

Every book is printed and shipped by Amazon, so you order it there, the same way you order any other paperback. Our links go straight to Amazon, with no affiliate codes. How this site counts visits is explained on our privacy page. You can also find us on Pinterest and read our articles.
