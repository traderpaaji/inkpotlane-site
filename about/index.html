About Inkpot Lane

Inkpot Lane is a small, independent publisher of paperback books that are meant to be used.

The coloring books are drawn with lines thick enough for small hands and shapes big enough to finish, one picture to a sheet. The planners and journals are built to be answered with a pen: fewer decorations, better questions.

Every book is printed and shipped by Amazon, so you order it there, the same way you order any other paperback. Everything here is a real book.

We also write articles: the stories behind the festivals our coloring books celebrate, and practical how-tos for the planners. You can also find us on Pinterest.
