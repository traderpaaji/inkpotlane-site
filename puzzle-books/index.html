These puzzle books for adults are all paperbacks you solve with a pencil, and every one has its answers at the back, so you always have a way to check your work. Where they differ is the kind of thinking they ask for, and how big the print is. This page is here to help you match the book to the solver.

If you like to reason it out

The Pennyfold Deduction Club is 80 logic grid puzzles set across one village year, from easy to fiendish. Each puzzle gives you a short story, a set of clues and a grid to mark, and the solutions at the back write out every step, not only the final answer. If you have never tried one, how to solve logic grid puzzles walks through the method.

The Twelve Cases of Christmas is for the solver who wants a story with the reasoning. It is twelve cases set in a snowbound village in the days before Christmas, and in each one you cross suspects off a list until one is left, with lists that grow from 40 suspects to 1,500. Christmas mystery books for adults has more on this kind of book.

If you want large print

Two books on this shelf are printed large throughout, answers included.

Large Print Cryptograms: The Village Detective's Casebook has 200 short coded notes: case clues, old sayings and curious facts, each written in a letter-for-letter code. The first cases give you three letters to start, and the help shrinks until the last cases give none. How to solve cryptograms explains the method, starting with the one-letter words.

Large Print Christmas Word Search: The Snowy Village Story has 90 word searches whose hidden messages, read in order, tell one Christmas story. It is a gentle, familiar puzzle in big letters, and Christmas activities for seniors has more ideas for the season.

For Christmas, or all year

Two of the four are Christmas books: The Twelve Cases of Christmas and the Snowy Village word search. The logic grids and the cryptograms work all year round.
