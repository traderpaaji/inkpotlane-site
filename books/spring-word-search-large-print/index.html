Spring Word Search Large Print for Seniors and Adults

A Cozy, Relaxing Puzzle Book of 90 Easy-to-Read Puzzles with Hidden Messages That Tell the Story of One Spring, from Crocus Buds to Early Summer

Ninety big, clear word searches for an open window and a cup of tea, whose hidden lines follow one spring from the last snow to the first ripe strawberry.

Who it's for

Adults and seniors who like big letters and a calm, cheerful hour by an open window. Every puzzle hides a short line from one spring seen from a window and a garden: the last snow slipping from the path, the first robin on the lawn, rain on the roof, blossom on the cherry tree, lambs in the lane, lilacs by the gate and, at last, the first ripe strawberry.

Ninety puzzles is about one for each day of March, April and May, or begin whenever spring finds you. It is a thoughtful gift for a spring birthday or for Mother's Day.

Inside the book

  • How to Play, with a page map of the levels and a small worked example of the hidden message.
  • 90 puzzles in three levels. Puzzles 1 to 30 are easy, with words running across, down and diagonally down to the right. Puzzles 31 to 60 are medium and add words that read backward and diagonally upward. Puzzles 61 to 90 are harder and may run in all eight directions.
  • A spring note on every puzzle page: a gentle prompt or a small, true fact about the season.
  • Themed word lists: seeds and soil, robins and nests, rain, blossom, bees, lambs, picnics, lilacs and more.
  • A hidden message in every grid. Read in order, the ninety messages become one quiet spring diary, "The Spring Window," printed in full at the back.
  • All 90 answers at the back, in large print, each with its hidden message.

How to use it

  • Start at Puzzle 1. The levels are gentle steps, and How to Play shows what changes at each one.
  • Read the leftovers. When every word is circled, read the unused letters from left to right and top to bottom, adding spaces and commas as you go. They spell that page's line of the spring diary.
  • Use a pencil. A thin loop is easy to erase and does not hide the letters around it.

Spring follows winter here too: the Winter Word Search follows one winter from the first frost to the first snowdrop, in the same large print and the same three levels.

Frequently asked questions

How big is the print?

Grid letters are 20 to 24 point, word lists are 16 point, and the answers are 16 to 18 point.

How hard are the puzzles?

They get harder in three steps of thirty: words that read forward, then backward and upward words too, then all eight directions.

Is it an Easter book?

No. It is about the season itself: gardens, birds, weather and the lengthening days, from the last snow to early summer.

What is the hidden message?

When every word is found, the letters left over spell a short line. Read in order, the ninety lines make one spring diary, printed in full at the back.

How big is the book?

8.5 x 11 inches, 122 pages, printed in black ink on white paper.
