A look inside

Who it's for

Anyone who wants the whole goal-planning system without a calendar deciding when they are allowed to begin. Dated planners are lovely in January and a little accusing by April, when a stack of untouched weeks sits at the front of the book. This edition has no printed dates, so you start on any Monday and a slow week never wastes a page.

It is the same planner as the 2027 dated edition: the vision pages, the 9-grid goal map, the goal ladders, the weekly spreads and the monthly reviews, with the dates left for you to write in.

How it works

  • Choose the one goal worth a year. A vision board, a life wheel, one word for the year and an ikigai page help you decide what matters most.
  • Break it down on the 9-grid goal map. One big goal in the centre, eight supports around it, sixty-four small actions of five to twenty minutes. Here is the method, step by step.
  • Write in your own dates. Each week starts on a Monday you choose. Skip a week and the next page simply becomes the next week.
  • Use a short weekly routine. Fifteen minutes on a Sunday is enough to keep the whole system alive. This is the routine we suggest.

Frequently asked questions

How many weeks does it have?

52 undated weekly spreads, Monday to Sunday, plus the monthly and quarterly pages.

When should I start it?

Whenever you are ready. That is the whole point of an undated planner: March, June or the day after your birthday all work equally well.

What is the difference from the 2027 edition?

Only the dates. The dated edition has 53 printed weeks for 2027; this one has 52 weeks you date yourself. We compare the two here.

How big is it?

8.5 x 11 inches, 214 pages, colour interior with original illustrations and a glossy cover.
