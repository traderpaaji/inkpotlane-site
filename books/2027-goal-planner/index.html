A look inside

Who it's for

Anyone who has started a year with a list of resolutions and quietly lost it by February. Most planners give you a calendar and leave you alone with your goals. This one connects the biggest thing you want to the smallest thing you will do today, and asks you to look at both every week: honestly, kindly, and on one page.

It is a paper planner, written in by hand. There are no apps to open and nothing to charge. The dated edition runs 53 Monday-start weeks, from Monday 28 December 2026 to Sunday 2 January 2028.

How it works

  • Choose the one goal worth a year. A vision board, a life wheel, one word for the year and an ikigai page help you decide what actually matters before you plan anything.
  • Break it down on the 9-grid goal map. One big goal in the centre, eight support goals around it, and sixty-four small actions of five to twenty minutes each. It is spread over four pages so every square is big enough to write in. Here is how the 9-grid method works, step by step.
  • Climb the goal ladders. Each support goal gets quarter milestones, twelve monthly mini-goals, a weekly habit and a daily ten-minute action, plus a plan for what could stop you.
  • Push three ladders at a time. Each quarter opens with a triage: push three ladders, keep the rest ticking with their daily action, pause any that must wait.
  • Live it week by week. Every week is a two-page spread: a top priority and four task lines for each day on the left, and on the right three big rocks from your quarter goals, ten-minute steps from your ladders, a habit grid, wins, gratitude, a brain dump and the one thing for next week.
  • Look back often. Every month has a review and a "this month, remembered" page, every quarter has an honest review, and the year closes with a review of what you built.

Frequently asked questions

What dates does the 2027 planner cover?

53 weeks, Monday to Sunday, from Monday 28 December 2026 to Sunday 2 January 2028, so the first week of January is fully covered at both ends of the year.

Do I have to fill in every box?

No. Use the whole system, or start with just the weekly pages and let the planner grow with you. Nothing breaks if you skip a section.

Should I get the dated or the undated edition?

If you want to start in January and like having the dates printed for you, choose this one. If you might start later, or tend to skip weeks, the undated edition never wastes a page. We compare the two honestly here.

How big is it?

8.5 x 11 inches, 216 pages, colour interior with original illustrations and a glossy cover.
