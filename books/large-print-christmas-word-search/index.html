A look inside

Who it's for

Anyone who loves a large print word search and a cozy Christmas, and would like the two together. Solve the 90 puzzles in order and their hidden messages tell one story: the snowy village of Pine Hollow getting ready for Christmas, from the first snowfall to Christmas Day.

Grandma Rose and Grandpa Tom get the cottage ready, their grandchildren Lucy and Ben arrive by train, and there is a dog named Pepper. Then the star for the village tree goes missing. It makes an easy gift for a grandparent, a stocking or a Secret Santa.

Inside the book

  • How to Play. A large-print guide to where each level, the answers and the story begin, how the story works, and a small worked example.
  • 90 puzzles in three levels. Easy puzzles 1 to 30 are 11 x 11 grids, with words running across, down and diagonally down to the right. Medium puzzles 31 to 60 are 12 x 12 and add words that read backward and diagonally upward. Harder puzzles 61 to 90 are 13 x 13 and use all eight directions.
  • Every puzzle page has a Christmas title, a FESTIVE NOTE, the grid, a word list in 16 point type, and two lines to write that page's part of the story.
  • The answers, pages 94 to 116, in large print: four to a page, with each part of the story printed under its grid.
  • The whole story, printed complete on pages 117 to 119, then a thank-you page from Pine Hollow.

How to use it

  • Solve in order. Each hidden message picks up where the one before it stopped.
  • Write each message down as you go. The two writing lines on every page let the story build up in your own hand.
  • Don't try to read ahead. The message letters are scattered through the grid, never more than 8 side by side, so the story only appears once the words are found.
  • Save the last pages for last. The complete story at the back is the reward for finishing.

Frequently asked questions

How big is the print?

The grid letters are about 20 to 24 point, the word lists are 16 point, and the answer key is in large print too.

Is it really one story?

Yes. The 90 hidden messages join into one story, told a line at a time. The answers print every part, and the complete story follows the answers.

Is every festive note a fact?

Many are checked facts, such as when electric tree lights were first shown. Others are gentle notes about the season.

Is it a religious Christmas book?

No. It is a secular Christmas of snow, lights, baking, carols, Santa and family.

How big is the book?

8.5 x 11 inches, 120 pages, printed in black ink.
